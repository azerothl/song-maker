# What’s new — v0.4.2

This release brings together Studio fixes and the new mix assistant.

## VST3 effects and instruments

- On Windows, each audio or MIDI track can have a VST3 effects chain with add, parameter, enable, order and removal controls. Inserts run in realtime during Studio playback and are included in rendered exports.
- **MIDI track settings → Choose a VST3 instrument** opens the piano-roll selector directly. Installed plugins are discovered automatically; manual search refreshes the list.
- During native recording in **Standard** mode, input monitoring can use a selected track’s inserts. The recorded take stays dry. This monitoring is unavailable in exclusive mode.
- The existing master effect keeps its offline preview/export rendering. Plugins and sound banks are installed separately; the VST3 host remains Windows only.

## Studio assistant

From **Studio → Mix assistant**, describe a gain or pan change, request a regular MIDI beat or ask for a piano line following an available melody.

The assistant shows a preview before **Apply to project**. Added tracks remain editable in the piano roll and appear immediately in the arrangement. History and undo are kept per project, including after restart. Stale proposals are rejected to preserve manual changes.

To follow a melody, select a MIDI voice or an audio track to transcribe. Without a usable source, the assistant explains what is missing. Audio transcription uses a Python environment with `basic-pitch==0.4.0` and `onnxruntime`; the script is embedded in the app.

## Playback, export and track settings

- Built-in or VST3 MIDI instruments and their inserts are included in mix and aligned-track exports. MIDI-only projects can be exported.
- Playback covers the whole arrangement even when a VST3 track is shorter; Pause also stops already scheduled MIDI notes.
- MIDI notes appear in Studio lanes. MIDI projects are recognized as arrangements.
- Track settings tabs stay readable, and track reordering offers a drag handle as well as keyboard arrows.
